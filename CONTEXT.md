# regulae domain language

This glossary names the linguistic claims regulae may make. It keeps a surface
relationship model distinct from the historical interpretations that may later
be built from it.

## Input

**Lect**:
A language variety, historical stage or other coherent form inventory compared
with another such inventory.
_Avoid_: Language, daughter language, source language

**Cognate set**:
A caller-supplied assertion that forms share relevant lexical history; regulae
does not establish that assertion.
_Avoid_: Discovered cognates, word pair

**Surface form**:
The segmented and normalized representation supplied for one form, including
any caller-supplied prosodic or morphological annotation.
_Avoid_: Phonetic truth, proto-form

## Model

**Surface relationship model**:
A structured account of how forms in the supplied cognate sets align and how
their segmental and suprasegmental values covary across lects.
_Avoid_: Sound-law model, reconstruction, family tree

**Alignment link**:
An association between one or more positions in one surface form and one or
more positions in another.
_Avoid_: Historical event, change

**Correspondence**:
A recurrent association among aligned surface segments or spans in two or more
lects, without a claim about direction, ancestry or cause.
_Avoid_: Reflex rule, sound law

**Conditioned correspondence**:
A correspondence whose outcome distribution differs between an attested
environment and its attested complement.
_Avoid_: Conditioned sound law, causal rule

**Environment**:
A conjunction of attested phonological, prosodic, positional or caller-supplied
morphological predicates associated with a correspondence.
_Avoid_: Cause, trigger

**Computational orientation**:
Which of a pair's two lects a search states its environment over. Both are
searched, and they ask different questions: only a lect that still shows the
conditioning contrast has an environment to state. Which lect that is says what
each lect preserved.
_Avoid_: Direction of change, ancestor, source language

**Contrast**:
The observations in the complement of an environment against which a
conditioned correspondence is measured.
_Avoid_: Exceptions, elsewhere law

**Decision list**:
An ordered surface description in which each later conditioned correspondence
accounts for observations left unresolved by earlier ones.
_Avoid_: Chronology, derivation

## Evidence and interpretation

**Search standing**:
A verdict that a selected surface association exceeds the reference level
reached by the same search under its stated null model.
_Avoid_: True rule, valid sound law, significance

**Corpus fit**:
The degree to which the supplied form pairings are more predictable under the
surface relationship model than under a stated comparison model.
_Avoid_: Proof of relatedness, genealogical verdict

**Historical hypothesis**:
A downstream interpretation that may group correspondences and propose
direction, chronology, inheritance, contact or causation.
_Avoid_: regulae rule
