# georgian_svan — findings

30 Kartvelian cognates descended from Proto-Kartvelian (CK).

## Correspondences recovered

Key patterns captured in the segment table and chunk promotion:

- **Geo `tʰ` ↔ Svan `ʃ` (context-conditioned)**: committed as
  `tʰ → ʃ / [vowel:+]_`. Classic Geo `t` : Svan `šd` cluster
  correspondence (`atʰi` ~ `jeʃd` "ten", `tʼili` ~ `ʃdim` "louse",
  `datʼvi` ~ `dəʃdw` "bear").
- **Geo `e` ↔ Svan `i`**: committed as a context-conditioned
  shift (`i → i / [voiced:+]_` bucket; also unconditioned
  `deda ~ di` "mother", `nen- → ena/nin` "tongue").
- **Geo `s` ↔ Svan `ʃ`**: `sva ~ ʃwe` "drink".
- **Ejectives preserved in both branches**: `tsʼida ~ tsʼid`
  "dirt", `tʼba ~ tʼob` "lake", `mkʼerdi ~ mətʃʼed` "chest".
- **Svan word-initial w-**: `otʰxi ~ woʃtʰxw` "four",
  `xutʰi ~ woxuʃd` "five", `ekʰvsi ~ usgwa` "six".
- **Consonant-cluster simplification**: Svan often reduces
  initial CC to C or V+C (`dzma ~ dʒimil` "brother",
  `zɣva ~ zugwa` "sea").

## What this validates

- The framework handles ejective-rich, cluster-heavy consonant
  inventories without special configuration.
- Svan's rich vowel system (əa-style realizations) doesn't
  confuse the aligner.
- Sparse data (30 cognates) still yields committed context
  splits — the discovery layer is not over-dependent on large N.
